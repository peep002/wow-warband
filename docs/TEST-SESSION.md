# Playtest session: Act I (phases 2-6 in one sitting)

The same checks as `docs/TEST-QUEUE.md` tests 2-6, in playing order. About an hour. Note anything
odd as you go (a screenshot of the window helps most); the numbers in brackets are the test-queue
steps, so results can be recorded there.

## Getting in
1. Desktop **ServerLauncher.exe**: Warband should say **Running** (it is). Click Warband's **Play**.
2. Log in as **PEEPOO**. Make a **new character**, any race and class except death knight.
3. In game, type `.gm off` (GM mode switches the lockdown off).

## The hamlet
4. You're in **Raven Hill** at **level 10**, wearing **green starter gear**, with a gold welcome line
   and a **banner button** on the minimap. [2.1, 6.1]
5. Walk ~110 yards out of the village: you're sent back. [2.5]
6. Click the minimap button: the **Warband** window. Check it looks like a proper WoW window, the
   tabs work, Escape closes it, and it can be dragged. [2.3]

## Your first heroes
7. **Tavern** tab: 6 founding recruits, free, at least one tank and one healer. Hover the green/red
   traits. Hire **4**. [3.1, 3.2]
8. **Roster** tab: call **3** heroes to your party. They appear beside you, level 10, geared, in
   your group (even across factions). Send one back, call them again. [3.3-3.5]
9. Talk to **Sergeant Marlow** (village centre) and **Maude Ashby** (west side): each opens the
   window on its tab. [2.4, 3.6]

## A mission
10. **Missions** tab: the act line ("main missions 0 of 4..."), up to 3 contracts, each listing its
    gold, renown and XP. **Embark** on one. [4.1, 2.7]
11. Fight to the target boss. Loot corpses: **coins vanish**, gear says "goes to the armory", cloth
    says "becomes materials". Kills give very little XP. [6.2, 4.2]
12. If a hero dies: "is down! 45 seconds..."; when the fight ends they stand up **wounded**. [5.1, 5.2]
13. Kill the boss: "Mission complete!", ~1.5-2.5 levels for everyone who went, gold and renown,
    home after 10 seconds. [4.2, 6.3, 2.8]

## Back home
14. **Armory** tab: your loot, with WoW tooltips and "Upgrade" marks. Click an item and give it to
    someone; try **Equip all upgrades**, **Sell junk**, **Sell**, **Salvage**. [6.4-6.6]
15. **Roster**: wounded heroes have a **Treat** button; missions counted. **Tavern**: 4 new recruits
    for gold. Board: new contracts. [5.5, 4.3, 3.7]
16. Embark again and click **Abandon mission** in the window: home, a quarter of the gold. [2.9]
17. Log out and back in: your party heroes come back by themselves. [3.8]

## If you have time
18. Play more missions: does the pace feel right? Is level 10 too hard for these dungeons? [4]
19. Let someone die for good (a wounded hero going down again): **Memorial** tab. [5.3]
20. Die yourself with nobody to save you: the mission fails and you wake up wounded at home. [5.4]

## What to tell me
- Anything broken, and anything confusing in the window.
- Whether dying feels fair (45 seconds, the "fight is over" rescue).
- Whether gold feels too tight or too loose.
